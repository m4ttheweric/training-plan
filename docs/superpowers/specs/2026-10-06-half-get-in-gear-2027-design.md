# Half marathon plan: Get in Gear 2027

Plan file: `plans/half-get-in-gear-2027.json`

## Brief

- **Race:** Get in Gear Half, Minneapolis, Sat Apr 24 2027 (confirmed). Flat river parkways, 15 min from home.
- **Goal:** 2:00-2:03 (~9:10/mi). Sub-2:00 is decided after the week 24 tune-up, not now.
- **Structure kept from the 10K plan:** 4 runs + 3 lifts. Mon long / Tue squat / Wed easy + strides + bench / Thu rest / Fri quality / Sat easy + deadlift / Sun rest.
- **Athlete:** 40, base 13-18 mi/wk, longest run 7.7 mi, 10K 55:14 (6.26 mi), 5K 26:11, ~6.2 h sleep. Soleus resolved: no calf-specific rules.

## Shape

- 28 weeks, Mon Oct 12 2026 to Apr 24 2027, as one plan.
- **Base (wk 1-12):** all easy except strides, hills (wk 5-7) and fartlek (wk 9-11). Cutback every 4th week (3:1).
- **Race build (wk 13-28):** threshold, then race-specific, peak, taper, race week. Cutback every 3rd week (2:1).
- **Volume:** 14 mi/wk grows to a 26.5 mi peak. The long run grows from 6 to 11 mi.
- **Taper:** the last 11-miler is 19 days out. Wk 27 cuts ~30%. Race week is 9 mi plus the race.

## Evidence behind the choices

| Choice | Source |
|---|---|
| 12-16 wk race build; 22-28 mi peak; 10-12 mi long run | Higdon Novice 2 / Intermediate 1; Strava/McMillan guide; Strava data on sub-2:00 finishers (median ~25 mi/wk) |
| Long run grows ≤ ~0.5 mi past the 30-day max | Frandsen et al. 2025 BJSM (single-session spikes >10%: HR 1.52-2.28) |
| No 10%-rule dependence | Nielsen 2014 JOSPT; Damsted 2019 JOSPT; systematic review (weak evidence for any threshold) |
| 2:1 cutbacks in the race build | Masters coaching consensus (RunnersConnect, TrainingPeaks). Coaching practice, not trial data |
| 1 quality day, ~80/20 easy/hard | Muñoz/Seiler 2014 (polarized beat threshold-heavy in recreational 10K runners) |
| Threshold first, race pace late; strides and hills throughout | Pfitzinger, Faster Road Racing |
| 2-week taper, 41-60% volume cut | Bosquet 2007 meta-analysis |
| Keep heavy lifting; no heavy legs the day before the long run | Blagrove 2018 (economy +2-8%); Schumann 2022 (small interference) |
| Sleep gate on quality days | de Jonge 2025 (poor sleepers ~1.8× injury risk) |
| 2:00-2:03 goal | Daniels VDOT ~36 from the 10K and 5K; Riegel; Vickers & Vertosick 2016 (low-mileage runners land at the slow end) |

## Known departures

- **Long run is ~41% of the week at peak.** Daniels caps it at 30% when weekly mileage is under 40. No 4-run half plan meets that cap. Mitigation: two rest days before every long run.
- **T sessions run ~2.5-3 mi.** Daniels caps T work at 10% of weekly mileage, which is ~2.2 mi here. Instead, this uses the common 20-25 minute tempo standard.
- **No VO2max intervals.** With one quality day, half-pace specificity wins. Strides cover top-end speed.
